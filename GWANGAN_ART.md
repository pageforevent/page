# 이전 광안대교 시안 04 (현재 사용하지 않음)

현재 화면은 landmark-art.js의 코드 기반 픽셀아트입니다. 아래 생성 그림과 프롬프트는 이전 시안 기록으로만 보관합니다.

첨부 사진을 구조 참고로 사용해 built-in imagegen으로 생성한 기본 일러스트 위에 자동차, 배, 잔물결 SVG 애니메이션을 별도로 얹었습니다. 기본 교량은 움직이지 않습니다.

웹 자산: `media/gwangan-v4.webp` (원본 PNG의 WebP 형식 변환).

시간·날씨 변화는 기본 그림의 색조와 기존 날씨 레이어로 표현합니다. 현재는 한 장의 낮 일러스트를 기반으로 하므로 밤의 실제 교량 조명 배치를 별도로 그린 상태는 아닙니다. 다른 명소는 확인 전까지 바꾸지 않습니다.

## 최종 생성 프롬프트

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a square illustrated base layer for an animated website postcard. Reference image is a GEOMETRY and PERSPECTIVE reference of the real Gwangan Bridge in Busan, not an edit target. Create a beautiful structurally coherent editorial travel illustration of exactly this suspension bridge and its coastal skyline. Draw the WHOLE bridge as one coherent object in one consistent three-quarter elevated perspective: a distant smaller white tower left of center and a larger nearer white tower at the right, ONE continuous two-level steel truss road deck running through the lower parts of both towers, sagging suspension cables attached to the tower tops with straight vertical hangers ending precisely at that deck, characteristic curved hourglass crossheads and arch-shaped openings like the reference, massive connected white lower supports standing on wide concrete island foundations in the water. The road deck must not rest on the tower tops, tower tops must be far above the roadway, no disconnected columns, no floating or mismatched geometry. The larger right-hand tower, lower legs and entire foundation must all be visible. Style: refined playful screen-printed illustration, tasteful kitsch, simplified solid colored shapes with subtle risograph grain, slim confident outlines, not a technical SVG diagram, not photorealistic, not a toy bridge. Palette: rich blue and teal sea, clean cream-white bridge with lilac shadows, cyan sky, muted lavender/mint/pink distant Busan skyline. Composition: square artwork, skyline in upper background, diagonal bridge across middle and lower-middle, open blue water in lower quarter, small quiet sky area upper left. No embedded sun, moon, clouds, weather, text, logos, labels, border, smiley, cars, boats or foreground foam; these will be animated later as separate UI layers. Mild paper texture. Accurate bridge silhouette and physically believable engineering are the primary requirement. Keep both tower crowns and both lower foundations inside frame with generous edge clearance.
